Waypoint Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026 (updated for Waypoint 1.7 and Waypoint Pro subscriptions; Waypoint was called Ballast before)

Waypoint is a portfolio tracker and backtesting app for Windows that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what information is collected when you use Waypoint, why, who handles it, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "the developer" mean the independent developer of Waypoint. "You" means the person using Waypoint.

The short version: your holdings, portfolios, backtests, results and settings stay on your PC and are never sent to us. To give you an account, we keep your username, email address and a few account details. When something breaks, Waypoint sends us a technical error report (you can turn this off). We don't sell or share your information, and we don't use advertising or tracking.

1. What stays on your computer

Waypoint does its work locally. The following are stored only in your Windows user folder (%APPDATA%\Ballast, which keeps its original name so nothing is lost when you update) and are never sent to us:

  • the holdings, share counts, prices paid, cash and accounts you type into My Dashboard
  • your portfolios, glidepaths, goals, settings and theme
  • backtest results, saved backtests, forecasts and logs, including the local error log (errors.log)
  • daily backups of your data and any exports you make
  • the market-data and economic-data caches
  • your conversations with the AI assistant (see section 4)
  • any API keys you add for Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude

Uninstalling Waypoint keeps this folder so you don't lose your work. Delete the folder yourself to remove it.

3. Who handles your information (service providers)

We use a small number of services to run Waypoint. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: stores accounts and passwords and handles sign-in, including Google sign-in.
  • Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and Gmail: run our account service, store the account list, feedback and error reports, and send verification-code emails. Waypoint emails come from the developer's Google account.
  • GitHub (Microsoft): hosts the Waypoint download page and updates. When Waypoint checks for or downloads an update, your computer connects to GitHub, which can see your IP address.
  • Yahoo Finance: prices, dividends and fund details are downloaded directly from your computer, not through us. Yahoo can see your IP address and which tickers are requested, under Yahoo's own terms. This includes the tickers in your holdings.
  • Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED): the economic indicators on the Recession risk page are downloaded directly from your computer. FRED sees your IP address and which public indicators are requested, nothing about you.
  • ntfy (optional, Pro): if you turn on phone notifications, Waypoint sends your alerts (for example "Today's buy: 2 VOO, 1 SCHD") to the free ntfy service under a private random topic, and the ntfy app on your phone shows them. They never include your balance or account details. Anyone who knows your topic can read them, so keep it private; turn this off in Settings at any time.
  • Ollama: if you set up Waypoint Agent, your computer downloads the free Ollama engine and the AI model from Ollama's servers once. After that the assistant runs offline on your PC.
  • Stripe and Link (payments): Waypoint Pro is sold through Stripe Managed Payments, with Stripe (shown to you as Link) acting as the merchant of record. When you start a trial or subscribe, you enter your email, payment details and billing address (country and, where needed, postal code, for sales tax and VAT) on Stripe's secure checkout. Stripe processes the payment, charges any tax, sends your receipts, handles payment support and disputes, and lets you change your card or cancel (on link.com or through the billing page Waypoint opens), under Stripe's and Link's own privacy policies. Stripe shares with us only what we need to run your subscription (listed in section 2), the email used at checkout and a customer ID. Waypoint's checkout carries your account ID so the subscription is matched to your account.

Google, GitHub and these providers may log technical information such as IP addresses when your computer connects to them. We don't use these logs to track you.

4. The AI assistant

You choose which assistant to use:

  • Waypoint Agent (default): runs entirely on your PC through Ollama. Your messages never leave your computer.
  • Grok, Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity (optional): if you connect one with your own API key, your messages, a summary of your latest backtest and your list of portfolios are sent to that company (xAI, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic or Perplexity) under its API terms, and billed to your own account with them.
  • News scans (optional): with one of those assistants connected, Waypoint asks it on a schedule (for example every two hours while the market is open) to search the news about the ticker symbols you hold. Only the symbols are sent, never share counts, amounts or account details. You can turn news scans off in Settings.
  • Updating your holdings in your own words (optional): when you ask an assistant to update My Dashboard (for example "I still have my Apple, but 20% is now AMD"), Waypoint sends it your message and your current holdings in that account: ticker symbols, share counts and their dollar values, so it can work out the change. With Waypoint Agent this stays on your PC. Nothing else from your account is sent, and nothing is saved until you confirm.

Some free tiers let the provider use your content to improve its products. Apart from the cases above, your holdings and account details are not sent. Don't type private information into the chat.

5. How we use your information

We use account information and error reports only to:

  • create and secure your account, verify your email and prevent fake or automated accounts
  • provide the features of your plan, give referral rewards and enforce the one-PC rule for paid plans
  • run your subscription: start the free trial once per person, switch Pro on and off, and remind you before a trial turns into a paid plan
  • find and fix problems in Waypoint
  • send service messages, such as codes, password resets and important changes to Waypoint or these policies
  • respond to feedback and support requests, and improve Waypoint
  • comply with the law and protect the rights and safety of users and the developer

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it for advertising or profiling. We will only send marketing emails if you have opted in, and every such email will include a way to stop them.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information: kept while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it from our account service and sign-in provider within 30 days.
  • Accounts that never verify their email: may be deleted at any time.
  • Error reports: kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Feedback: kept until it is no longer useful or you ask us to delete it.
  • Event logs: kept for up to 24 months for security, then deleted.
  • Subscription records: kept while your account exists. Our payment provider keeps its own transaction and tax records for as long as the law requires (often several years), even after you cancel or delete your account. A note that a PC has used a free trial is kept so trials can't be repeated.
  • Some information may remain briefly in backups held by our providers, and we may keep information longer where the law requires it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • See the account information we hold about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete your account and its information
  • Get a copy of it
  • Object to or limit how we use it, where the law gives you that right
  • Turn off automatic error reports in Settings, and choose not to connect any online AI assistant

To ask for any of these, use Account → Feedback & reviews in Waypoint with the type "Question", or reply to any email from Waypoint. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the account holder. We'll respond within 30 days, or sooner if your local law requires it. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

You can stop using Waypoint at any time. Your local data stays on your PC unless you delete it.

8. Security

Passwords are handled by Google Firebase and never stored by us. All connections to our account service use encrypted HTTPS. Account sessions on your PC are digitally signed so they can't be altered. Access to the account list and error reports is limited to the developer. API keys you add are stored only on your PC. No system is completely secure, so use a strong, unique password and keep your PC protected.

9. Children

Waypoint is intended for adults and is not directed at children. You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live) to create an account. If we learn that a child has created an account, we will delete it.

10. International users

Our service providers may store and process information in the United States and other countries. Those countries may have different data protection laws from yours. By using Waypoint, you understand that your account information is processed there.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when paid plans launch. We'll change the effective date above, and for significant changes we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. Continuing to use Waypoint after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy or your information, send a message from Account → Feedback & reviews in Waypoint, or reply to any email from Waypoint.
